## Archive authority defects and lessons

### 1. Conflicting archive identity

Two sidecars for the same v0.33 archive basename have different SHA-256 values. Without the actual archive bytes, neither digest can prove the historical archive identity. Both remain quarantined.

### 2. Newer filename/version does not prove stronger authority

The v0.35 control/validation layer can be newer than clearly materialized ZIP custody. Catalog records must keep version/claim and physical-custody truth separate.

### 3. Derived current-status files became stale

Historical `CURRENT-PROGRAM-STATUS` / `CURRENT-MODULE-STATUS` drifted from the authoritative queue. Future packets are therefore disposable/generated views; the catalog/source registry is the persistent evidence layer.

### 4. Research method improved after earlier closures

The stronger `MASTER-MODULE-RESEARCH-METHOD-001` requires archaeology, comparison against strong external systems, primary specs, frontier research, user voice, failure analysis, architecture tournaments, explicit ADOPT/ADAPT/REJECT/DEFER decisions, cross-module authority checks and qualification-before-implementation. Previously closed modules should receive a **material delta gate**, not wholesale reopening.

## Required disposition taxonomy

Every recovered asset should end in one of these machine-readable reuse states:

- `REUSE_AS_IS`
- `REUSE_WITH_REQUALIFICATION`
- `REFERENCE_ONLY`
- `MIGRATION_INPUT`
- `DELTA_RESEARCH_REQUIRED`
- `SUPERSEDED_HISTORY`
- `DUPLICATE`
- `QUARANTINE_CONFLICT`
- `IDENTITY_UNPROVEN`
- `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED`

Research status, implementation status and qualification status must remain separate dimensions.

## Audit conclusion

The user's proposed direction is justified, with one critical correction: **A-01 should process and verify the evidence, not decide the system taxonomy.**

The durable target is a federated source registry + system catalog + asset/capability catalog + provenance/trace graph + per-system packets. A-01 supplies deterministic identity, schema/invariant validation, extract/manifest receipts and exact-subject qualification. Chat startup consumes the resulting packet before broad research.

This converts the historical archive from a pile of potential rediscovery into a traceable reuse base while preserving the current architecture's authority boundaries.
