# FOUNDATION-SPINE-CONSTITUTION-001

Status: CANONICAL TARGET ARCHITECTURE

## Mission

The Foundation & Spine is the shared operating substrate that turns an authorized user intention into durable, governed, resource-bounded, recoverable work without taking semantic ownership away from specialist systems.

It must make one piece of work traceable from intent to outcome, survive interruption, prevent stale or unauthorized actors from mutating truth, constrain AI/tool agency, preserve artifacts and evidence, and recover without guessing.

## Product-level outcome

A user should be able to ask System Master to perform work ranging from a short answer to a multi-day autonomous project. The system must preserve what the user meant, plan it, admit it, route it, place it, execute it durably, invoke models/tools/specialist systems safely, preserve outputs and evidence, expose truthful control to the user, and recover correctly from failure.

## Constitutional laws

1. ONE TRUTH OWNER PER CONCEPT. A canonical fact may have projections and caches, but never competing authoritative stores.
2. INTENT IS REVISIONED, NEVER SILENTLY REWRITTEN. Planning may change; the user's governing goal and constraints change only through an explicit new revision.
3. DESCENDANTS CANNOT EXPAND AUTHORITY. Child work inherits ceilings for permissions, privacy, scope, cost, resources, deadline and delegation.
4. PLAN IS NOT PERMISSION. A planner or model may propose actions; separate authorities decide admission, placement and effects.
5. HARD CONSTRAINTS PRECEDE OPTIMIZATION. Ineligible routes/executors are filtered before scoring preferences.
6. DURABILITY BEGINS BEFORE ASYNC EXECUTION. Once admitted, meaningful work identity and state must survive process/device interruption.
7. LEASES AND FENCES ARE MONOTONIC. A stale executor cannot regain mutation authority by continuing to run.
8. EXACTLY-ONCE EXTERNAL EFFECTS ARE NEVER ASSUMED. Use idempotency, effect receipts, reconciliation and compensation where possible.
9. UNKNOWN IS A REAL STATE. An uncertain commit, stale evidence or missing response is not guessed into success or failure.
10. UI AND TELEMETRY ARE PROJECTIONS, NOT BUSINESS TRUTH.
11. EVIDENCE CLAIMS MAY NOT EXCEED THEIR EVIDENCE CLASS. Portable, hosted, A-01, native, human, external and production evidence are distinct.
12. ARTIFACT IDENTITY IS DIGEST-BOUND. Mutation creates a new artifact/version; it does not rewrite historical identity.
13. SECRETS ARE REFERENCES/CAPABILITIES, NOT PROMPT OR LOG CONTENT.
14. EFFECT AUTHORITY IS COMMIT-TIME AUTHORITY. Old planning approval does not authorize a changed effect.
15. ROUTE AND PLACEMENT ARE DIFFERENT. Routing selects a qualified capability/provider path; placement binds an exact executor.
16. RESOURCE ADMISSION AND FINAL RESERVATION ARE DIFFERENT. Early admission approves a bounded workload; final reservation binds resources to the selected route/placement.
17. RECOVERY MAY NOT DUPLICATE SOURCE-OF-TRUTH OWNERSHIP. Recovery coordinates existing authorities.
18. SHARED INFRASTRUCTURE DOES NOT ABSORB DOMAIN SEMANTICS. Learning owns learning truth; Book owns book truth; Documents owns generic document semantics; future admitted systems retain their own semantics.
19. AI CANNOT SELF-GRANT CAPABILITY, BUDGET, CREDENTIALS OR RELEASE STANDING.
20. CHANGE INVALIDATES EVIDENCE WHEN ITS SUBJECT CHANGES. Changed code, schema, route, model, dependency, policy, target or ownership requires the evidence appropriate to that change.

## What Foundation & Spine owns

Shared control, identity, contracts, intent, work identity, orchestration, resource admission, routing, execution placement, durable runtime, transport, context assembly, model/tool gateways, effects, shared artifact/data custody, evidence, observability, recovery coordination, shared security/privacy/crypto, provider/dependency governance, AI safety controls, rights decisions, shared UX control projections, and change/migration/release/operator governance.

## What it does not own

It does not own curriculum/mastery semantics, manuscript/canon/author truth, generic document-format semantics, programming-domain correctness, media-domain correctness, research-knowledge truth, spreadsheet-domain truth, or any future specialist semantic truth merely because those systems use shared runtime services.

## Shared specialist overlays

Creative Fabric and similar cross-domain specialist services are consumers/providers of capability, not spine control authorities. They may contribute planning information or quality evaluation but cannot bypass Keel, admission, routing, placement, effect authorization, data ownership, evidence or specialist correctness.

## Architecture versus implementation

This constitution specifies what must exist. It does not assert that every authority is already implemented. Current implementation standing is maintained separately in FOUNDATION-SPINE-IMPLEMENTATION-BASELINE-001.md.
