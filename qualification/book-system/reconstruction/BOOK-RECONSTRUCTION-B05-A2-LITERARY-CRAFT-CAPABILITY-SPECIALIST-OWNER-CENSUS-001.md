## 6. Craft Academy / source-rights owner census

The historical Literary Craft Academy is decomposed as follows.

### B02-owned source authority inputs

B02 remains responsible for:

- source acceptance identity/digest;
- custody and rights/private-source acceptance evidence;
- normalized projection acceptance;
- external/native/provider extraction fidelity evidence and blockers;
- provenance establishing which source/version/projection is being used.

B05 may not weaken or replace these standings.

### B05-owned literary craft responsibilities

B05 may own:

- literary task-fit classification for an already admitted/source-identified craft source;
- conditioned craft principle/distillation records that contain mechanism, conditions, counterconditions, reader effect, diagnostic signals, failure modes, interactions, revision-transform descriptions **as knowledge**, confidence, scope and provenance;
- literary retrieval against a bounded diagnostic target;
- uncertainty/abstention when craft evidence is weak or inapplicable;
- conflict checks preventing external craft guidance from overriding B03 canon, B04 reader evidence, B08 voice standing, B10 author intent/protected language or local task purpose.

### B06 boundary inside craft records

A craft record may describe a generic/abstract revision transform as knowledge, but B05 may not apply it to produce candidate manuscript text. Application/generation is B06.

### Source rights / imitation law

- no named-author imitation target;
- no full-text admission by B05;
- no rights inference from prestige, catalog presence or usefulness;
- unresolved rights remain unresolved;
- literary task-fit cannot convert an unadmitted source into an admitted source;
- B05 retrieval returns derived conditioned craft intelligence/provenance, not unauthorized source text.

Craft Academy/source-rights rows unaccounted: `0`.

## 7. B05 vs B06 — diagnosis / revision boundary

B05 may output:

- strengths;
- limitations;
- risks;
- contradictions;
- confidence and evidence dependencies;
- purpose relevance;
- preservation/voice/collateral risk observations;
- diagnostic scope limits;
- bounded opportunity nominations;
- `NO_ACTION`, `ABSTAINED` or `BLOCKED` outcomes;
- abstract craft mechanisms/transforms as retrieved knowledge.

B05 may **not** output:

- replacement manuscript prose;
- candidate text;
- a selected revision ambition level as execution authority;
- an applied transform;
- a canonical mutation command;
- a claim that a revision is superior.

Those cross into B06 and/or B07.

## 8. B05 vs B07 — diagnosis / evaluation boundary

B05 answers: **What appears to be working, limiting, risky, contradictory or worth examining, and why?**

B07 answers: **Given an original/candidate/qualified subject, what independent evaluation/calibration standing does the evidence support?**

B05 diagnostic confidence is not evaluation calibration. B05 may not convert its own diagnosis into proof that a generated candidate improved the work.

Any future B05 model/specialist accuracy claim must be calibrated under B07-governed evidence rather than self-certified inside B05.

## 9. B05 vs B08 — local craft / governing voice boundary

B05 owns local craft observations such as diction, cadence, dialogue mechanics and potential convergence signals.

B08 owns governing state such as:

- stable author/project/book/character voice identity;
- explicit/project preference standing;
- `PROTECT | RANGE | CHALLENGE | SUPPRESS` trait disposition;
- `VOICE_DEGRADATION | VOICE_EVOLUTION | VOICE_NEUTRAL_CHANGE | VOICE_UNCERTAIN` standing;
- accepted/rejected-decision learning;
- project-to-project or long-horizon homogenization standing.

B05 must keep observations and B08 standing structurally distinct.

## 10. B05 vs B09 — local craft / distant coherence boundary

B05 may diagnose bounded passage/scene/chapter craft effects and local interactions needed to understand the current unit.

B09 owns distant/global coherence that requires long-range multi-unit/book-scale synthesis as its principal object: distant setup/payoff balance, book-wide patterning, long-range structural coherence and other global relationships not necessary for bounded B05 diagnosis.

B05-C may define exact allowed diagnostic scopes, but may not erase this owner boundary.

## 11. Opportunity adjudication ownership

B05 owns the pre-revision opportunity ledger/adjudication because it is still diagnosis, provided all of these laws hold:

- multiple findings that identify one underlying opportunity are deduplicated;
- correlated evidence does not inflate confidence as if independent;
- disagreement/contradiction remains visible;
- source-owner conflicts fail closed;
- strength evidence remains visible rather than being discarded in a problem-only report;
- `NO_ACTION` remains valid;
- ranking is bounded triage, not a universal prose-quality score;
- opportunity output contains no replacement text and no evaluation winner claim;
- revision execution remains B06.

The historical default maximum of three opportunities is retained as a useful recovery constraint but is **not yet a B05-C frozen cardinality**. B05-B must adjudicate whether it remains exact, configurable under a hard cap, or is replaced by another bounded rule.

## 12. B05 currentness / invalidation dependency census

Future B05 diagnostic artifacts must be invalidated or blocked when an exact dependency they consumed is no longer current.

### Dependency classes B05 must account for

1. **B02 source/custody/projection identity**
   - source acceptance ref/digest/currentness;
   - normalized source projection ref/digest/currentness where consumed;
   - rights/custody standing relevant to retrieved craft-source evidence.
2. **B03 Story Bible / canonical knowledge**
   - Story Bible ref/digest/currentness;
   - exact semantic truth refs/digests used by diagnosis;
   - upstream invalidation impact where relevant.
3. **B04 reader projection**
   - exposure projection ref/digest/currentness where consumed;
   - understanding projection ref/digest/currentness where consumed;
   - exact dimension/lens evidence refs used by a diagnostic finding.
4. **B05 craft-knowledge substrate**
   - craft record/source projection identity/digest;
   - task-fit/relevance policy version/digest;
   - specialist/diagnostic contract version/digest.
5. **B01/provider admission**
   - exact provider subject/admission identity/digest/currentness for any provider-produced diagnostic evidence.
6. **B08 voice/preference state**
   - only where a B05 finding consumed voice constraints/evidence; absence does not permit B05 to synthesize B08 standing.
7. **B10 author/editorial constraints**
   - explicit author intent, protected-language or user-decision refs/digests/currentness where consumed.
8. **scope/source anchors**
   - bounded diagnostic scope ref/digest and exact source anchor identities/digests needed to reproduce the evidence relation.

### Layering law

A changed dependency invalidates only B05 artifacts that actually consumed that dependency. B05 must not force unrelated B03/B04/B08/B10 state to become stale merely because a craft finding changed.

B05 invalidation is downstream and non-mutating: it can mark/recompute B05 diagnostic evidence but cannot mutate B02 source custody, B03 Story Bible, B04 reader projections, B08 voice state or B10 author decisions.

- real-book diagnostic accuracy;
