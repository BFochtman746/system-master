### Decision

The detailed B03 semantic graph lives in `STORY_BIBLE`, not duplicated in `CANON_MANIFEST`.

`CANON_MANIFEST` remains a governed canonical manifest/index surface under B00. It may reference the active Story Bible version and canonical objects, but it must not contain a second independently mutable copy of entities, events, temporal relations, relationships, arcs, promises, motifs, themes or open questions.

The Story Bible is therefore the canonical semantic knowledge object for B03. Canon Manifest remains manifest/index authority, not a parallel semantic store.

## Historical materializer disposition

### Decision: KEEP-BUT-REFACTOR

The historical materializer's strongest laws are retained:

- every candidate is bound to exact source/anchor evidence;
- contested evidence cannot silently become a single asserted winner;
- alternatives/unresolved interpretations are preserved;
- duplicates merge provenance rather than confidence votes;
- no raw manuscript text is persisted merely to coordinate knowledge;
- validation occurs before a materialized candidate can proceed;
- insufficient evidence yields sparse/rejected results rather than invented canon.

The historical fixed global numeric confidence floors are **not** current canonical authority. B03-C must replace them with a versioned acceptance/calibration policy whose thresholds may depend on claim class, provider/model subject and calibrated evidence class. No threshold may override author authority, currentness, exact-source binding, contest preservation or private/right restrictions.

## Missing semantic decisions

### Narrative duration and frequency

**Decision: BUILD as B03 temporal semantics.**

B03 owns descriptive story-time duration/extent and recurrence/frequency semantics because they are part of the canonical temporal/event model. They must remain evidence-bearing and may be unknown/ambiguous. B03 does not turn duration into pacing quality; pacing belongs B04/B05/B09 as appropriate.

### Relationship state

**Decision: BUILD first-class relationship identity/state.**

A relationship is not merely an arc label. B03-C must define stable relationship identity, participant refs, evidence/provenance, status/current state and change/event linkage. Relationship arcs then reference relationship identity.

### World state / preconditions / consequences

**Decision: BUILD bounded fact/change semantics, not a simulation engine.**

B03 should support evidence-bearing state assertions and event precondition/effect/change relationships sufficient for continuity and causal reasoning. It must not become an unrestricted world simulator or infer absent state transitions merely to complete a graph.

### Theme and subtext

**Decision: BUILD first-class hypothesis semantics.**

Theme/idea/subtext records are descriptive, provenance-bearing hypotheses with confidence/status/contest handling. They are not literary quality scores, author-intent claims or revision instructions. B05/B07/B09 may evaluate literary effectiveness; B03 stores admitted knowledge state.

### Promise

**Decision: BUILD first-class promise identity/lifecycle.**

Promise is retained as a B03 responsibility because the reconstruction blueprint explicitly places promises in B03. Promise records must be linkable to introducing evidence, relevant events/questions/setup-payoff objects and resolution/payoff evidence, with OPEN/PARTIAL/FULFILLED/INTENTIONALLY_UNRESOLVED/INVALIDATED-type lifecycle semantics to be frozen in B03-C. B04 may model reader expectation generated by a promise; it does not own canonical promise identity.

## Candidate -> Story Bible admission boundary

### Decision

There are three distinct layers and none may be collapsed:

1. **Projection/proposal evidence** — B02 recovered candidates and provider/model outputs. Noncanonical.
2. **B03 materialized knowledge candidate** — deterministic Book-owned normalization/validation/invalidation result bound to exact source/manuscript/projection identities. Still noncanonical.
3. **Governed Story Bible version** — immutable B00-admitted `STORY_BIBLE` object version with exact predecessor/currentness/provenance and any required author authority. This is the canonical object layer.

B03 cannot write the active Story Bible pointer directly. Final version registration/pointer movement uses B00 authority.

A contested interpretation may be admitted as explicitly contested/alternative/unresolved knowledge without pretending one winner is canonical fact. Choosing one contested interpretation as definitive author/canon truth requires applicable author authority.

## Provider boundary

### Decision: DELEGATE execution, retain Book acceptance

`BOOK.LITERARY.BUILD_NARRATIVE_STATE` remains a provider projection/evidence capability. Provider outputs may propose anchors, entities, events, temporal/causal/knowledge relations, arcs and related hypotheses.

Provider/model output has:

- no canonical-write authority;
- no Story Bible active-pointer authority;
- no author-decision authority;
- no ability to override stale source/currentness failures;
- no authority to turn confidence into truth;
- no historical PASS transfer.

B03 owns deterministic schema validation, materialization rules, candidate standing, invalidation, admission preparation and calibration acceptance criteria.

## Invalidation/currentness decision

B03 invalidation is transitive but bounded by provenance dependencies.

B03-C must freeze these laws:

- every admitted semantic object/claim has exact provenance dependency refs;
- a changed/removed source or manuscript anchor invalidates directly dependent semantic records;
- invalidation propagates through dependent causal, relationship, arc, promise, setup/payoff, motif/theme and open-question objects;
- unrelated records are retained;
- invalidation creates a successor Story Bible candidate/version rather than mutating historical Story Bible versions;
- no stale dependent object retains current standing merely because its ID still exists;
- unresolved/alternative states remain explicit across successor versions unless evidence/authority resolves them.
