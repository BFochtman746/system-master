## What changed

The independent literary-evaluation system now has an explicit reliability boundary for the failure modes identified in the current research pass. Structured judge-trial evidence can be checked for repeated-run inconsistency, position effects, generator-identity/self-preference effects, score-range sensitivity, epistemic-uncertainty-marker sensitivity and controlled belief-contrast sensitivity. A surface-style preference cannot override preservation or story-function failure. TIE and ABSTAIN remain valid outcomes and are never converted into manufactured consensus.

Reliability evidence contains candidate IDs/outcomes rather than candidate prose, emits no literary-quality score and never authorizes promotion. A `STABLE` result is only eligibility to continue through the existing STEP-I literary comparison gates; it is not authority to accept a revision.
