`PRESENT_BUT_COARSE__EXPAND_CURRENT_OWNER`

2. motive / value pressure

5. intention / commitment

### Hard boundary

## Research basis incorporated in this new revision

Current narrative-planning research continues to model character goals, beliefs, intentions and relationships as distinct state needed for believable character action. Character-consistent dialogue research supports structured persona/knowledge grounding rather than simple persona prompting. Pragmatic analysis of fictional dialogue supports treating speech acts, politeness/face, interruption and silence as meaningful interaction state. Creativity research supports context-dependent novelty/effectiveness rather than surface novelty metrics. Recent literary-model studies show difficult subtext remains an error-prone area and automated judgments can diverge from author judgment. Narratology-informed current work continues to distinguish temporal order, duration and frequency, while recent structural pacing work separates nonlinearity, information latency, surprise and closure. Book-length evaluation research supports multi-resolution local/global evaluation rather than one monolithic score.
