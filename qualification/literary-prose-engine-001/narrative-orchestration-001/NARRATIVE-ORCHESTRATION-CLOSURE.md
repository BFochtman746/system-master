## Boundary now established

The Literary Prose system can now represent long-range narrative organization over canonical event/entity/claim IDs without retaining manuscript text. The layer supports:

- narrative-function assignments with asserted/alternative/unresolved status;
- setup/payoff lifecycle;
- open-question lifecycle including intentionally unresolved states;
- character, relationship, plot, motif and idea arc objects;
- arc beats tied to canonical events;
- provenance and confidence;
- precise invalidation when dependent narrative-state events/entities/claims change.

The validator explicitly does **not** require a three-act structure, a midpoint, a climax at a prescribed position, closure of every question, or any other universal plot template. An arc may plateau, reverse, reopen, terminate early or remain intentionally open without being labeled defective. Global orchestration is descriptive evidence, not prose-quality authority.
