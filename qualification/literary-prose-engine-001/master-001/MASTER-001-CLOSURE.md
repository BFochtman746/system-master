## Binding decisions

- Voice is not frozen at the current manuscript baseline.
- Repetition alone never proves a trait deserves preservation.
- Voice traits are adjudicated as `PROTECT`, `RANGE`, `CHALLENGE`, or `SUPPRESS`.
- Voice is hierarchical: author -> project -> book -> POV/character -> local passage.
- `VOICE_DEGRADATION` is distinct from legitimate `VOICE_EVOLUTION`.
- Manuscript version/maturity state is first-class development evidence.
- Accepted and rejected revisions both become chronological preference evidence.
- External references teach transferable craft, never named-author imitation.
- No universal prose score is permitted.
- Original text is retained on unresolved uncertainty.
- Writer and evaluator remain logically separated.
- Homogenization and overoptimization are explicit failure modes.
- STEP-B through STEP-L are implementations of the master plan and may not silently redefine its invariants.
