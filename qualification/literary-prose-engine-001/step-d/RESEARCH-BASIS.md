# STEP-D Research Basis

This qualification uses public metadata, abstracts, published standards pages, license notices, and repository/package metadata. It does not persist external book/article full text.

## High-authority source families

- Editors Canada, Professional Editorial Standards 2024 — scope, audience, meaning, voice/style/POV/tone/register, structural and stylistic editing, accuracy and permissions.
- Open University OpenLearn — advanced critical-reading-as-writer and fiction pedagogy; host courses are Creative Commons but embedded third-party materials are explicitly excluded, requiring component-level provenance.
- OpenStax Writing Guide with Handbook — CC BY-NC-SA open textbook; useful for POV/characterization/rhetoric fundamentals, not a sole literary-fiction authority.
- LitBank — 100 English fiction works, about 210k annotated tokens, entities/events/coreference/quotation attribution; CC BY 4.0.
- BookNLP — MIT-licensed code tooling for book-length entity/coreference/quotation analysis; code license does not license its training corpora.
- Yang et al. 2023 DOC — detailed outline control improves long-story generation coherence/controllability in its tested automated setting.
- Shi et al. 2025 — systematic position-bias study supports position swap/repetition controls for model judges.
- Text-style-transfer evaluation research — supports multidimensional separation of style change, content preservation and naturalness, with explicit cautions about automatic metric validity.
- Nature Human Behaviour 2026 research briefing — large-scale evidence that AI writing assistance can compress linguistic diversity while preserving meaning, supporting anti-homogenization safeguards.
- Narrative transportation / neurocognitive poetics research — supports multi-factor reader-response models involving attention, emotion, imagery and story-world processing.
- Project Gutenberg and Standard Ebooks rights policies — public-domain candidate sources remain work/edition/jurisdiction specific.
- CoAuthor — valuable human-AI process data, but held in quarantine because this qualification did not establish a sufficiently explicit reusable dataset license from the source page.
- PERSUADE — CC BY 4.0 argumentative-writing corpus; admitted only for argument/discourse measurement due task mismatch with literary fiction.

## Research rule

Source authority, legal permission, and task match are independent. No source becomes a general literary authority because it is famous, open, highly cited, or easy to download.
