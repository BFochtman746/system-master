STEP-H implements the first bounded revision-generation layer. It consumes qualified passage state, admitted specialist opportunities, and qualified foundry transforms; emits immutable candidate alternatives; preserves a mandatory unchanged control; and refuses to decide that its own prose is superior.

- every candidate set includes `LEVEL_0_NO_CHANGE` with the original text unchanged;
- nonzero ambition levels map to STEP-E edit scopes and cannot exceed `maximum_edit_scope`;
- only `ADMIT` opportunities can drive candidate generation;
- only qualified STEP-G transforms can drive candidate generation;
- transform scope and counterconditions are enforced;

- original text is never overwritten by candidate generation;
- the default candidate budget is three nonzero alternatives plus the unchanged control;
- writer/generator quality claims are prohibited;
- every nonzero candidate remains `UNEVALUATED_PENDING_STEP_I`;
- deterministic candidate IDs bind passage state, ambition, opportunities, transforms, and candidate text.

## Pre-evaluation preservation boundary

STEP-H may detect and reject obvious preservation failures. It may also carry review flags such as voice identity, character integrity, narrative distance, register, or collateral effects forward to STEP-I. It may not convert the absence of an obvious failure into a claim that a revision is better.
