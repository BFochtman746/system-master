## Runtime behavior frozen by S06

S06 makes Learning-effectiveness evaluation governance executable while preserving owner boundaries:

1. Learning owns the educational evaluation question, exact Learning use, protocol identity, and bounded interpretation.
2. Curriculum/version identity is pinned for the instructional, tutoring, assessment, prompt, model, policy, scaffold, adaptive-policy, and tool context needed for interpretation.
3. Generic statistics/fairness-analysis machinery remains owned by Shared Assurance or Analytics rather than becoming a second Learning authority.
4. Evaluation outputs cannot write or overwrite canonical mastery, retention, transfer, assessment, goal, or self-regulation truth.
5. Comparator, design, assignment integrity, identifying assumptions, target population/context, intended claim, outcome families, and analysis rules remain explicit.
6. Retention, transfer, independence, learning-efficiency, self-regulation, experience, burden, and adverse-effect outcomes remain semantically distinct.
7. Immediate performance cannot silently masquerade as durable retention, transfer, independence, or universal effectiveness.
8. Supported success cannot silently masquerade as independent performance when independence is required.
9. Authorized accommodation remains distinguishable from prohibited or interpretation-changing assistance.
10. Task completion, accepted AI output, prompt reduction, engagement, preference, and perceived helpfulness cannot by themselves establish educational effectiveness.
11. Consequential evaluations require adverse-effect/burden review, and material adverse signals remain visible rather than being averaged away.
12. Sensitive subgroup analysis is bounded by authorization, necessity, minimum-sample, uncertainty, and re-identification protections; raw chat/camera/voice/physiological/device telemetry cannot be mined for inferred sensitive traits.
13. Statistical reporting preserves sample count, design unit, analysis unit, point estimate, uncertainty, missingness, and contamination standing where applicable.
14. Statistical significance is not treated as educational importance, and no universal effect-size threshold is asserted.
15. Evaluation packages preserve exact protocol/intervention/comparator/instrument/data/code/environment provenance needed for governed reproducibility.
16. Corrections/reanalysis are successor lineage; prior results remain preserved rather than destructively overwritten.
17. Downstream policy consumption binds the exact evaluation result/version and cannot directly create or revoke individual learner mastery.
18. Population-level effectiveness evidence cannot be projected as an observed fact about one learner.
19. Certification, licensing, hiring, and job-eligibility decisions are explicitly outside Learning-effectiveness authority.
20. Material model/provider, tutoring, prompt, curriculum, adaptive-policy, scaffold, tool, or assessment changes trigger coverage/effectiveness review and fresh software qualification as applicable.
21. Result standing is constrained to the frozen S06 standing set: `PLANNED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `EXPLORATORY`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, `SUPPORTIVE`, `ADVERSE_SIGNAL`, `SUPERSEDED`, `NOT_GENERALIZABLE`.
22. `SUPPORTIVE` remains bounded to the exact evaluated protocol, versions, population/context, outcome definitions, and analysis; it is not universal proof.
23. One positive evaluation cannot be represented as proof that System Master is educationally effective.
24. Passing software qualification cannot be represented as educational-effectiveness or measurement-validity evidence.
