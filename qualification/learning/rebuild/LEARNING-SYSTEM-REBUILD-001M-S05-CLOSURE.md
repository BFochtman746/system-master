## Runtime behavior frozen by S05

S05 makes the assessment/model-governance boundary executable while preserving predecessor authority:

1. Curriculum remains the canonical owner of assessment definitions; Learning snapshots definition identity/version for learner-specific execution without rewriting Curriculum truth.
2. Learning owns learner-specific assessment attempt, submission, review, finalized-result, challenge, and external-delivery state.
3. Attempt creation, submission, raw scorer output, provider delivery, and learner challenge cannot directly mint mastery.
4. Scorer/model output remains an observation until owner-valid Learning finalization requirements are satisfied.
5. Finalized assessment results are admitted only as evidence inputs to the frozen S03 mastery projector; S05 introduces no second mastery writer.
6. Assessment attempts pin exact definition, course, skill, criterion, rubric, and scoring-policy identity needed for later interpretation.
7. Submission identity remains distinct from attempt identity, score-observation identity, finalized-result identity, and mastery projection identity.
8. AI scoring is policy-governed: disallowed AI is blocked, advisory AI cannot masquerade as final score truth, and finalization-eligible AI requires exact model/configuration/prompt identity plus required Assurance standing.
9. Provider metadata and model self-confidence cannot substitute for owner-valid Assurance standing.
10. Calibration, fairness, drift, use scope, model version, rubric version, response type, language, and domain scope constrain automated finalization where policy requires them.
11. Material scorer disagreement and high-consequence use require governed human review rather than silent averaging or highest-confidence selection.
12. Human review preserves original observation lineage, evidence references, reason codes, and adjudication standing without requiring hidden chain-of-thought.
13. Integrity signals and AI-detector-like flags are review inputs only; they cannot silently invalidate an attempt or evidence.
14. Authorized accommodation, permitted tools, instructional scaffolding, and prohibited assistance remain distinguishable for evidence interpretation.
15. Score correction/regrade is append-only successor lineage. Historical result/evidence bytes remain preserved; superseded evidence is excluded only from future active projection inputs.
16. External/provider score delivery remains fail-closed as pending/review state; successful transport is not score finalization and duplicate delivery cannot create duplicate semantic score truth.
17. Finalized result, admitted evidence input, owner event, and operation receipt are committed transactionally before downstream reprojection.
18. Consequential result explanations expose bounded definition/rubric/scorer/model/review/assistance standing and reason codes without exposing or depending on hidden chain-of-thought.
19. Software qualification remains distinct from psychometric validity, educational effectiveness, population validity, certification, licensing, hiring, or job-eligibility evidence.
20. `LRN-069 / LRN-EXT-002` authoritative cross-domain competency equivalence remains unresolved and deny-by-default.
